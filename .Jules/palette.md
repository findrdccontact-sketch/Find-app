## 2025-05-18 - Tooltips on Icon-Only Buttons for Screen Readers & Web Hover
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons (like favorite heart icons on cards) lack accessible labels unless `tooltip` or `semanticsLabel` is explicitly provided in Flutter `IconButton` widgets.
**Action:** Always provide explicit descriptive `tooltip` parameters for interactive `IconButton`s to improve accessibility for screen reader users and web/desktop hover states.
